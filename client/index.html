MY WORLD

HOW TO PLAY

  • W A S D — Move
  • SHIFT — Run
  • SPACE — Jump
  • Mouse drag — Look around
  • E — Interact (shops, players)
  • ESC — Open menu

ABOUT

My World is a small 3D open-world multiplayer game set in a Kerala-style town. Walk around, visit the tea shop, meet NPCs, and chat with other players online.
